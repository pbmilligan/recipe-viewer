Bacon Butter bean Soup

Calories: 386
Serves: 1

INGREDIENTS
- 2 rashers of bacon (54g, 116 cal)
- 1 tsp olive oil (27 cal)
- Half small onion (30g, 11 cal)
- Half leek (50g, 11 cal)
- Half carrot (40g, 14 cal)
- 1 stalk celery (1 cal)
- Half can of butter beans (200g, 206 cal)

METHOD
1. Finely chop all veg.
2. Fry bacon in oil for 2 min.
3. Add veg and cook for 5 min, adding a splash of water if it sticks.
4. Add butter beans and half a pint of water (250ml) and simmer for 20 min.
5. Season, blend or mash. For 4: * 8 rashers of bacon * 1tbsp  olive oil * 2 small onion * 2leek * 2 carrot * 4 stalk celery * 2 can of butter beans
