Borlotti Bean Bolognaise - 180 calories

INGREDIENTS
- (Instructions for two people)

- Cooking oil spray
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 100g button mushrooms, sliced
- 1tsp mixed herbs
- 400g tin tomatoes
- 1tbsp tomato puree
- Pinch caster sugar
- 400g can borlotti beans, drained and rinsed
- Salt and pepper

METHOD
1. Heat oil in a large pan and gently fry the onion, garlic and pepper for 3 minutes until softened. Add the mushrooms, herbs, sugar, tomatoes, tomato puree and sugar and cook for a further 3 minutes. Add beans, the cover and simmer for 20-30 minutes until the sauce has reduced. Season and serve.

NOTES
Used black-eyed beans instead of borlotti beans

SOURCE
Fast Cook, p26.
