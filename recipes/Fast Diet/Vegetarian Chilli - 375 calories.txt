Vegetarian Chilli - 375 calories

Serve: with 2 tbsp cooked wild brown rice (80g wet/35g dry, 113 calories).

INGREDIENTS
- (Instructions for one person: ingredients for two)

- 2 garlic clove, crushed
- Olive oil
- 1 red chilli pepper, finely chopped
- Cumin, pinch
- 8 small mushrooms
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tin kidney beans
- Rice to serve

METHOD
1. Fry a clove of garlic (3 calories) and half a fine-chopped red chilli in a tsp olive oil (27 calories).
2. Add a pinch of cumin and 1 large or 4 small chopped mushrooms (20g, 3 calories) and cook for 5 minutes, adding water if it sticks.
3. Add half a tin of chopped tomatoes (200g, 32 calories) and half a tin of kidney beans (200g, 200 calories), stir and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. do for 8
