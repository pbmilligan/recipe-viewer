Warm Lentil and Avocado Salad with Feta and Rocket

Calories: 385 per serving
Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 2 red onions, peeled and cut into eighths
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 250g cherry tomatoes
- 2 ripe avocados
- 50g wild rocket
- 2 x 410g tins green lentils, drained
- 200g low-fat feta or Greek style cheese, crumbled

METHOD
1. Preheat the oven to 200c, fan 180, gas 6.
2. Put the onions in a roasting tin and pour over the vinegar and 1tbsp water. Season and roast for 15 mins, stirring halfway through. (I add a little olive oil at this stage if its looking a little dry).
3. Add tomatoes and cook for a further 10-12 mins until they burst.
4. Peel and cut the avocados into bite-sized pieces. Put into a large bowl with the rocket, drizzle over the olive oil and season.
5. Remove the tomatoes and onions from the oven and add the lentils, stirring well. (The heat of the tin will warm them through). Add the lentil mixture to the salad and mix in gently. Sprinkle over the feta and serve.
