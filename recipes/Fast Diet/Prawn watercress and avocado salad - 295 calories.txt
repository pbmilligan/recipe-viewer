Prawn, watercress and avocado salad - 295 calories

INGREDIENTS
- (Instructions for one person)

METHOD
1. Mix 28g watercress (6 calories) with 140g cooked prawns (139 calories), half an avocado (72g, 137 calories), half a red onion (30g, 11 calories) chopped and 1 tbsp capers (2 calories).
2. Dress with white wine vinegar.
3. Try with lettuce instead of watercress
