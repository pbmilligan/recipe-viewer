Green lentil and mint - 224 calories

Serves: four

INGREDIENTS
- 250g green lentils, rinsed
- 800ml cold water
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 white onion, thinly sliced
- salt and pepper
- mint and parsley to serve

METHOD
1. Put lentils in a pan with cold water and bring to the boil.
2. Skim off froth, then stir in the turmeric.
3. Reduce heat and simmer, uncovered, for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally until you have a puree. Add a little boiling water if too thick.
4. Heat oil in a small frying pan and fry cumin seeds for a minute, then add onion and sauté for 5 minutes until softened.
5. Add onion mix to cooked lentils.
6. Season and serve topped with plenty of mint and parsley.
