Red Lentil Tikka Masala - 218 calories

Serves: 4
Calories: 218 per person so can serve with pitta or something
Serve: with a dollop of yoghurt.

INGREDIENTS
- Masala paste
- 2 tsps garam masala
- 1.25 - 1.5 tsps hot chilli powder or 0.5 tsp EXTRA hot chilli powder
- 2 tsps smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 2cm root ginger, peeled and grated
- 1 tbsp groundnut oil (we use sunflower)
- 2 tbsps tomato puree
- salt and pepper
- handful fresh coriander, finely chopped

- Curry
- 1.5 tbsps groundnut oil
- 1 red onion, diced
- masala paste as made from ingredients above
- 1 garlic clove, crushed
- 1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 250ml vegetable stock
- 200g red lentils, rinsed
- 4 generous handfuls of young spinach leaves, washed (200g)
- 2 tbsps low fat natural yoghurt

METHOD
1. Fry onion in oil until softened, around 3-4 min. Add garlic and cook for a further minute. Add masala paste ingredients and cook to release flavours. Add tomatoes and veg stock and bring to boil. Add lentils, reduce heat and simmer for 20–30 mins until lentils are soft. Remove from heat and add spinach leaves, allowing them to wilt in the warmth. If necessary, loosen with a little more hot stock.
