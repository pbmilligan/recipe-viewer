Skipper's Soup

Calorie: count: 142
Serves: One

INGREDIENTS
- 25g good-quality chorizo
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 200g tinned haricot beans, drained
- 300ml chicken stock
- Pinch of smoked paprika
- Salt and Pepper

METHOD
1. Chop the chorizo into small chunks and heat gently in a heavy-bottomed pan.
2. When it has released some oil, add the onion and fry gently until softened.
3. Add beans and stock and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Whizz with a hand blender until smooth. Add the paprika, check seasoning and serve.

SOURCE
The Fast Diet Recipe Book, p. 175.
