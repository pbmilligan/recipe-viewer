Curried Lamb with Apples and Bananas

Serves: 6-8

INGREDIENTS
- 1tbsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- 1 tbsp olive oil (approx.)
- 1kg trimmed stewing lamb, cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) cubes
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp minced gingerroot
- 2 tsp turmeric
- 1 cinnamon stick piece (2 inches/5 cm)
- 2 black cardamom pods, crushed
- 0.5 tsp cracked black peppercorns
- 1 cup beef or chicken stock
- 1 or 2 long red or green chile peppers, minced, or 1/4 tsp (1 mL) cayenne dissolved in 1 tbsp (15 mL) boiling water
- 3 apples, peeled, cored and thinly sliced
- 2 bananas/plantains , thinly sliced
- 0.25 cup finely chopped cilantro or parsley leaves

METHOD
1. Press the SAUTE/SEAR button and then the START/CANCEL button. Heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with the lid on.
2. Add half the oil and heat for 1 minute.
3. Add lamb in small batches and cook until lightly browned; remove and set aside.
4. Add onions to pan and cook, stirring, until softened, about 3 minutes.
5. Add garlic, gingerroot, turmeric, cinnamon stick, cardamom, peppercorns and reserved cumin and coriander and cook, stirring, until spices release their aroma, about 1 minute.
6. Return lamb to removable cooking bowl and add stock; mix well.
7. Press START/CANCEL button to end the saute setting.
8. Cover with lid. Press the SLOW COOK HIGH BUTTON and then the START/CANCEL button. Cook for 3-4 hours or select SLOW COOK LOW setting for 5-6 hours.
9. Discard cinnamon stick and cardamom pods. Add chile peppers, apples and bananas, in batches, stirring to incorporate each batch before adding the next. Cover and cook on HIGH for 30 minutes, until fruit is tender and hot. Garnish with cilantro.
9. Serve with brown basmati rice.
