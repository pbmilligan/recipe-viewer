Potato and Bacon Gratin

Serves: 2-4

INGREDIENTS
- 3 tbsp butter
- 6 tbsp bechamel sauce
- 500g potato, diced
- Bacon
- Gruyere chesse for topping

METHOD
1. Heat the oven to 180ûC / 350ûF / Gas 4. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a pan. Add the potato and bacon, turning them until coated and the bacon is browned. Use the last tablespoonful of butter to grease a gratin dish, then add the bacon and potato and spread it out: it should be no more the two pieces deep, and loosely packed. Season generously with sea salt and black pepper, then drizzle over the bechamel sauce. Top with chees and bake for an hour, until well-browned.

NOTES
Many variations.
1. Use 500g pumpkin, 2 sage leaves, chopped, and whipping cream.
2. Tomato and courgette, sauteed with garlic in a wide pan until dryish, then baked with a scattering of breadcrumbs.
3. Any vegetable. Sautee first to remove excess liquid. If using tomatoes precook other ingredients or allow extra time in oven as tomatoes harden the other veg. Moisten with stock, bechamel or cream. Top with breadcrumbs or cheese (add any toppings sparingly).
