Chicken Stir Fry

Serves: 1

INGREDIENTS
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 garlic clove, peeled and chopped
- 2 chicken thighs, chopped
- 0.5 red pepper, de-seeded and sliced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 30g mangetout
- 100ml chicken stock

METHOD
1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan or wok and sautŽ the garlic and chicken, until browned all over. Add the pepper to the pan and stir-fry for 2-3 minutes.
2. Stir in the soy sauce, mangetout and chicken stock, and continue to stir-fry over a medium-high heat for 3-4 minutes, or until the chicken is thoroughly cooked through.

NOTES
Doubled up the amounts; Added straight-to-wok noodles for the last minute; Added one shallot with the garlic.
