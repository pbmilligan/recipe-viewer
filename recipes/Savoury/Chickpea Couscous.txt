Chickpea Couscous

INGREDIENTS
- 10oz dry couscous
- 500ml Vegetable stock
- 1 orange Pepper
- 1 yellow pepper
- 1 courgette
- Half a red onion
- half tin chickpeas
- Olive oil
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- salt & pepper if required

METHOD
1. Preheat oven to 180
2. In a bowl, place couscous.
3. In a jug, make up stock and add the cumin, coriander and cinnamon and stir until dissolved. Pour over couscous, then cover tightly with cling film and leave for 5-10 mins until cooked and liquid absorbed.
4. meanwhile de-seed peppers and cut into quarters. Cut the half onion into half again and slice the courgette lengthways into 2 halves. Place into a roasting tin and drizzle with olive oil. Put into the oven and cook for about 10-15 mins until veg starting to go soft and pepper skins beginning to blister. Take out of the oven when soft and leave to cool slightly.
5. When couscous ready, add a little olive oil (about 1-2 tbsps) and fluff through with a fork. Add salt and pepper if desired and stir in chickpeas.
6. Peel skins off peppers and dice all the roasted veg (small) and add to the couscous.
7. Mix together and serve
