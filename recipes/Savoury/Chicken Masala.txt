Chicken Masala

Serves: 15
Serve: with a dollop of yoghurt.
Serve: with rice, naan, pappadums, mango chutney, spiced cauliflower, peas
Serves: 15

INGREDIENTS
- Masala paste
- 7.5 tsps garam masala
- 3.75-4.7 tsps hot chilli powder
- 7.5 tsps smoked paprika
- 3.75 tsp ground cumin
- 3.75 tsp ground coriander
- 7.5cm or 22.5g root ginger, peeled and grated
- 3.75 tbsp groundnut oil (we use sunflower)
- 7.5 tbsps tomato puree
- salt and pepper
- handful fresh coriander, finely chopped

- Curry
- 4.7 tbsps groundnut oil
- 3.75 red onion, diced
- masala pasta as made from ingredients above
- 3.75 garlic clove, crushed
- 3.75 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 938ml chicken stock
- 2.25kg Chicken breast, diced (have 2.425kg)
- 7.5 tbsps low fat natural yoghurt

For mild one do half quantities or even less for chilli and ginger and garam masala:

METHOD
1. Fry onion in oil until softened, around 5 min. Add garlic and cook for a further minute. Add chicken and paste and cook to release flavours. and brown chicken. Add tomatoes and chicken stock and bring to boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 30-40 mins.
