Vegetable Curry

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 45ml/3tbsp Oil
- 2 Onions, sliced
- 5ml/1tsp Chilli powder
- 5ml/1tsp Coriander
- 25g/1oz Root ginger, grated
- 2 Aubergines, sliced
- 175g/6oz Mixed Beans
- 100g/4oz Potatoes, cubed
- 225g/8oz Canned tomatoes, chopped

METHOD
1. Heat the oil and fry the onions until just browned. Add the chilli powder, coriander and ginger and fry for 2 minutes. Add the aubergines, peas and potatoes and fry for 5 minutes. Stir in the tomatoes and season to taste with salt and pepper. Bring to the boil. cover and simmer for 30 minutes until the vegetables are tender.

NOTES

Original recipe had peas instead of mixed beans

SOURCE
The Classic 1000 Recipes, Wendy Hobson (p.206)
