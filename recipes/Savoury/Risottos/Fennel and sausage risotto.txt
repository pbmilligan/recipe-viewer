Fennel and sausage risotto

Serves: 4
Takes: 1 hr
Calories: 464 per serving

INGREDIENTS
- Freezable

- 6 cups (1.2l) vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 440g pack Tesco Finest pork, fennel and chilli sausages
- 1 tsp crushed chillies
- 2 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 fennel bulb, fronds removed and sliced, bulb diced
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 400g risotto rice
- 180-250ml white wine
- 85g pecorino/parmesan, finely grated
- Black pepper

- Sauté setting

METHOD
1. Press the SAUTÉ|SEAR button and then START|CANCEL to heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with lid on.
2. Remove lid, add olive oil, heat for 1 minute.
3. Add the chillies and fennel seeds and cook for 1 min until fragrant.
4. Add the onion, garlic and fennel, cover with lid and cook, stirring occasionally for 4 minutes, until onion has softened.
5. Squeeze the sausages from their skins, breaking up the meat with a spoon. Cook for 10 mins until lightly golden.
6. Add rice, mix well, cover with lid and cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Stir in wine, and cook uncovered for 3 minutes or until all wine is absorbed.
8. Press the START|CANCEL button to stop cooking. Risotto setting
9. Stir in stock, cover with the lid, press the RISOTTO button and then START|CANCEL to start cooking.
10. When it switches to the KEEP WARM setting, which will take about 20–30 minutes, remove the lid and stir through 55g grated  cheese and black pepper. Cover and leave on WARM for 5 minutes.
11. Divide the risotto between bowls and top with the remaining cheese.

Original Method

Add 1 tbsp oil to a high-sided casserole dish over a high heat. Squeeze the sausages from their skins, breaking up the meat with a spoon. Cook for 10 mins until lightly golden. Transfer to a plate with a slotted spoon. Turn the heat down to medium and add the remaining oil. Add the chillies and fennel seeds and cook for 1 min until fragrant. Add the onion, fennel (reserving the fronds) and garlic and cook for 4 mins until beginning to turn translucent. Return the sausage meat to the dish.
Meanwhile, warm 1.5ltrs stock in a saucepan over a low heat.
Add the rice to the casserole dish and toast for 2 mins, stirring, then add the wine or extra stock, and use a wooden spoon to scrape any stuck bits from the bottom of the pan. Reduce the heat to low. Add the warm stock, 2 ladlefuls at a time, allowing the rice to completely absorb the liquid before adding more. Cook for 26-28 mins, stirring regularly, until the rice is cooked through.
Remove from the heat and add 55g pecorino and a crack of black pepper, then cover and leave to rest for 2 mins. Transfer half (about 1kg) to a bowl, allow to cool for a maximum of 30 mins, then cover and transfer to the fridge for up to 24 hrs to use in the Mediterranean stuffed peppers (see tip, below).
Divide the risotto between bowls, top with the remaining pecorino, the fennel fronds and another crack of black pepper. Serve immediately.
