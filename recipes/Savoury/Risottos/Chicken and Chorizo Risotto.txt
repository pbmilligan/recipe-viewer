Chicken and Chorizo Risotto

Serves: 6

INGREDIENTS
- Olive oil
- 200g chorizo sausage, diced
- 650g chicken breast or thigh, diced
- 1.5 onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 300 g arborio rice
- 150ml white wine
- 2 cans diced tomatoes
- 600ml chicken stock
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp red chilli flakes
- 60g parmesan, grated
- fresh parsley, chopped

- Sauté setting

METHOD
1. Press the SAUTÉ|SEAR button and then START|CANCEL to heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with lid on.
2. Remove lid, add oil, heat for 1 minute.
3. Add the onion, cover with lid and cook, stirring occasionally for 3 minutes, until onion has softened.
4. Add the sliced chorizo, chilli flakes and paprika and cook until browned and crispy. Add the diced chicken and garlic and cook until the chicken is browned on all sides.
5. Add rice, mix well, cover with lid and cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in wine, and cook uncovered for 3 minutes or until all wine is absorbed.
7. Press the START|CANCEL button to stop cooking. Risotto setting
8. Stir in tomatoes and stock, cover with the lid, press the RISOTTO button and then START|CANCEL to start cooking.
9. When it switches to the KEEP WARM setting, which will take about 20–30 minutes, remove the lid and stir through grated parmesan cheese and parsley. Cover and leave on WARM for 5 minutes.
