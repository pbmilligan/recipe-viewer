Chicken, Lemon and Chickpea Risotto

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 medium carrots, finely chopped
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- Inner few ribs of a head of celery, including as many of the leaves as possible, finely chopped
- 4 cups chicken stock (720ml)
- Bundle of fresh thyme
- 2 skinless chicken breasts, in bite-sized pieces
- 350g arborio risotto rice
- 120ml Dry white wine
- Salt and freshly ground pepper
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 60g Parmesan, finely grated

- Sauté setting

METHOD
1. Press the SAUTÉ|SEAR button and then START|CANCEL to heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with lid on.
2. Remove lid, add oil and heat for 1 minute
3. Add the chopped vegetables and stir. Cover and cook for at least 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, or until they're really soft and sweet, but be careful not to brown them too much.
4. When the vegetables are well cooked, stir in the chicken and cook until opaque.
5. Add rice, mix well, cover with lid and cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in wine, and cook uncovered for 3 minutes or until all wine is absorbed.
7. Press the START|CANCEL button to stop cooking. Risotto setting
8. Stir in stock and throw in a few stems of thyme. Cover with the lid, press the RISOTTO button and then START|CANCEL to start cooking.
9. When it switches to the WARM setting, which will take about 20–30 minutes, remove the thyme and stir through half of the grated parmesan cheese and the chickpeas. Cover and leave on WARM for 5 minutes.
10. Stir in the lemon zest and juice and serve with the remaining parmesan.
