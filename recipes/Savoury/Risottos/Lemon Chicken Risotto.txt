Lemon Chicken Risotto

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 medium carrots, finely chopped
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- Inner few ribs of a head of celery, including as many of the leaves as possible, finely chopped
- 2 skinless chicken breasts, in bite-sized pieces
- 350g arborio risotto rice
- 120ml Dry white wine
- Juice and zest of one lemon
- 4 cups chicken stock (720ml)
- 50g freshly grated parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon fresh chopped basil, plus extra for topping
- 1 tablespoon fresh chopped parsley, plus extra for topping
- Salt and freshly ground pepper
- lemon wedges for serving

- Sauté setting

METHOD
1. Press the SAUTÉ|SEAR button and then START|CANCEL to heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with lid on.
2. Remove lid, add oil and heat for 1 minute
3. Add the chopped carrots, celery, onions and garlic and stir. Cover and cook for at least 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, or until they're really soft and sweet, but be careful not to brown them too much.
4. When the vegetables are well cooked, stir in the chicken and lemon and cook until opaque.
5. Add rice, mix well, cover with lid and cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in wine, and cook uncovered for 3 minutes or until all wine is absorbed.
7. Press the START|CANCEL button to stop cooking. Risotto setting
8. Stir in stock and add basil and parsley and quartered lemon. Cover with the lid, press the RISOTTO button and then START|CANCEL to start cooking.
9. When it switches to the WARM setting, which will take about 20–30 minutes, stir through the parmesan and leave to warm for 5 minutes.
