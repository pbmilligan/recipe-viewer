Butternut Squash and Bacon Risotto

INGREDIENTS
- 1kg butternut squash, peeled and cut into bite­size chunks (I didn’t weigh, just used 1 butternut squash)
- 1­-2 tbsp olive oil
- 900 ml vegetable stock
- 50g butter or 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion
- 300g risotto rice
- 1 small glass white wine
- 50g parmesan
- 180g Tesco Finest Smoked Pancetta sliced but bacon would be fine

METHOD
1. Before you make the risotto, heat oven to fan 200C. Toss the squash in 1 tbsp oil. Scatter into a shallow roasting tin and roast for 20-30 mins until it is brown and soft.
2. While the squash is roasting, prepare the risotto in the risotto maker. Saute setting lid on 3 min.
3. Add half the butter or a tbsp oil and leave for 1 min with lid on.
4. Stir in the onions and pancetta and sweat gently for 8-­10 mins until soft but not coloured, stirring occasionally.
5. Stir the rice into the onions until completely coated in the oil, leave for 2­-3 min with lid on, stirring occasionally.
6. Pour in the wine and simmer with lid off until totally evaporated (3 min).
7. Add the stock and start risotto function.
8. When the squash is cooked, mash it to a rough puree.
9. When the risotto is just done, stir though the puree, then add the cheese and butter and leave to rest for a few mins.
