Risotto with sausage and red wine

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 2 1/4 pints  chicken, beef or veal stock 5.6
- 60g butter 150
- 1 large brown onion, peeled and chopped 2.5
- 400g Good quality sausages, meat removed from casings 1kg
- 250g Risotto rice 625
- 120ml red wine 300ml
- handful chopped flat leaf parsley
- 60g freshly grated parmesan 150
- salt and pepper

METHOD
1. Bring the stock to a gentle simmer in a large saucepan. Meanwhile, heat 40g of the butter in a large, heavy-based saucepan. Add the onion and cook, stirring, over a moderate heat for 3 minutes. Turn up the heat a little and add the meat. Cook, stirring, until it colours, breaking up any lumps with a fork. Add the rice and stir gently for 2 minutes. Add the wine, stir, and allow most of the liquid to evaporate. Add the stock, a ladleful at a time, to the rice mixture. Stir frequently and add more stock as it is absorbed. The rice will take 18-25 minutes to cook - when ready it should be soft but retain bite. If the stock runs out before the rice is cooked, continue with simmering water. Remove from the heat and add the parsley, parmesan and the remaining butter. Season to taste. Cover and allow to rest for 5 minutes before serving.

NOTES

Used chicken stock. Rice took about 22 minutes after starting to add the stock.

Risotto plus settings

4 cups chicken, beef or veal stock
60g butter
1 large brown onion, peeled and chopped
400g Good quality sausages, meat removed from casings
250g Risotto rice
120ml red wine
handful chopped flat leaf parsley
60g freshly grated parmesan
salt and pepper

Sauté setting
1. Press the SAUTÉ|SEAR button and then START|CANCEL to heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with lid on.
2. Remove lid, 40g butter, heat for 1 minute or until butter has melted.
3. Add the onion, cover with lid and cook, stirring occasionally for 3 minutes, until onion has softened.
4. Add the meat. Cook, stirring, until it colours, breaking up any lumps with a fork.
5. Add rice, mix well, cover with lid and cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in wine, and cook uncovered for 3 minutes or until all wine is absorbed.
7. Press the START|CANCEL button to stop cooking.

Risotto setting
8. Stir in stock, cover with the lid, press the RISOTTO button and then START|CANCEL to start cooking.
9. When it switches to the KEEP WARM setting, which will take about 20–30 minutes, remove the lid and stir through grated parmesan cheese, remaining butter and parsley. Cover and leave on WARM for 5 minutes.
