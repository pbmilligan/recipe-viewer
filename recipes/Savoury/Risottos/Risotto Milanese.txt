Risotto Milanese

Serves: 4-6

INGREDIENTS
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 40g butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 350g arborio risotto rice
- 1 cup white wine
- 4 cups chicken stock (720ml)
- Salt and freshly ground pepper
- 0.5 cup grated Parmesan cheese

- Risotto plus settings

- Sauté setting

METHOD
1. Press the SAUTÉ|SEAR button and then START|CANCEL to heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with lid on.
2. Remove lid, add oil and and half the butter, heat for 1 minute or until melted.
3. Add onion and garlic, cover with lid, cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until onion has softened.
4. Add rice, mix well, cover with lid.
5. Cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in wine, cover and simmer for 2-3 minutes or until wine has been absorbed.
7. Press the START|CANCEL button to finish sauté setting. Risotto setting
8. Stir in chicken stock, mixing well. Cover with lid and press the RISOTTO button and then START|CANCEL to start cooking.
9. When it has automatically switched to the WARM setting, which will take about 20–30 minutes, remove lid and stir through remaining butter and Parmesan.
10. Serve immediately topped with additional Parmesan.
