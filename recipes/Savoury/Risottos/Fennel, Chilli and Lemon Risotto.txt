Fennel, chilli and lemon risotto

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- (Note: quantities and method for use with Risotto Plus machine)

- 40 g butter
- 1 garlic clove, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp fennel seeds, ground
- 0.5 tsp dried red chilli flakes
- 1.5 large fennel bulbs, finely chopped
- 70g Parmesan, finely grated
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 300g arborio rice
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 4 cups hot chicken stock
- 1 unwaxed lemon, finely grated zest and juice

- Sauté setting

METHOD
1. Press the SAUTÉ|SEAR button and then START|CANCEL to heat removable cooking bowl for 3 minutes with lid on.
2. Remove lid, 30g butter, heat for 1 minute or until butter has melted.
3. Saute the garlic, fennel seeds and chilli flakes for 1-2 minutes.
4. Add the fennel and onion, cover with lid and cook, stirring occasionally for 7-8 minutes, or until fennel has softened.
5. Add rice, mix well, cover with lid and cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Stir in wine, and cook uncovered for 3 minutes or until all wine is absorbed.
7. Press the START|CANCEL button to stop cooking. Risotto setting
8. Stir in stock, cover with the lid, press the RISOTTO button and then START|CANCEL to start cooking.
9. When it switches to the WARM setting, which will take about 20–30 minutes, remove the lid and stir through grated parmesan cheese. Cover and leave on WARM for 5 minutes.
10. Stir in the lemon zest and juice and serve.
