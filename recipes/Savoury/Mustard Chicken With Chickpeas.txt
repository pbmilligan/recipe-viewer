Mustard Chicken With Chickpeas

Serves: 2

INGREDIENTS
- 1tsp Dijon mustard
- 2tsp balsamic vinegar
- 2tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 4 chicken thighs (bones in)
- 400g tin chickpeas
- 2tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Juice and grated zest of 1 lemon
- 3tbsp chopped parsley

METHOD
1. Heat the oven to 220degC (gas mark 7). Combine the mustard, balsamic vinegar, olive oil and one clove of garlic and bush the chicken thighs with the mixture. Season well. If you have time, marinate the chicken in the mustard mixture for a couple of hours before cooking, as this will add to the flavour.
2. Heat a frying pan over a medium heat and, when hot, add the chicken, skin side down. Sear for 3-4 minutes, then turn it over. Remove the pan from the heat and baste the chicken with any remaining marinade. If the frying an has a heatproof handle, transfer it to the heated over; otherwise, transfer the chicken and place in the oven. Roast for 15-20 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked and golden.
3. While the chicken is cooking, drain and rinse the chickpeas. Place them, with the extra virgin olive oil, remaining garlic and the lemon juice and zest in a saucepan over a gentle heat and warm thoroughly. Season to taste with salt and freshly ground black pepper. Just before serving, stir the parsley through. Serve the warm chickpeas with the chicken.

NOTES
"You can make this dish with chicken breast if you prefer white meat."
