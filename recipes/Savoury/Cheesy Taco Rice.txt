One Pot Cheesy Taco Rice

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- Approx per serving: 466 calories, 38g protein

- 500g 4% fat mince
- 300g basmati rice (dry weight)
- 760ml beef stock
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 20g tomato purée
- 40g grated mozzarella

METHOD
1.	Fry the mince in a large pan over medium-high heat.
2.	Add the taco seasoning and mix well.
3.	Wash the basmati rice, then add it to the pan.
4.	Pour in the beef stock and add the tomato purée. Stir everything together.
5.	Reduce the heat, cover, and simmer until the rice is fully cooked.
6.	Sprinkle the grated mozzarella evenly over the top.
7.	Cover again and let it melt for 1–2 minutes.
8.	Serve into 4 portions and enjoy
