Spiced Cauliflower

Serves: 8

INGREDIENTS
- 5 tbsp vegetable oil
- 5g ginger, finely chopped
- 1.6 tsp chilli flakes (used 0.25 for 200g cauliflower)
- 1.6 tbsp cumin seed (used 0.5 for 200g cauliflower)
- 1.6 tsp turmeric (used 0.5 for 200g cauliflower)
- 1kg (after trimming and removing leaves) cauliflower
- , broken into pieces

METHOD
1. Heat the oil in a big pan or wok with a lid, then add the ginger and spices. Swirl everything around for a few secs until the spices are aromatic. Reduce the heat, then stir in the cauliflower and some seasoning. Put the lid on and cook for 10 mins or until softened, stirring occasionally.
