Philadelphia Stuffed Chicken

Serve: simply with fresh seasonal vegetables.

INGREDIENTS
- 4 skinless chicken breasts
- 100g Philadelphia Garlic and Herbs
- 2 tbsp fresh basil leaves, shredded
- 12 pancetta, slices

METHOD
1. Pre-heat the oven to 200 C, gas mark 6. Place the chicken on a chopping board breast side down. Open out the fillet and spread a quarter of the Philly over each chicken breast. Fold the fillet back over and wrap each with 3 pancetta slices. Pop into a non-stick baking dish and roast in the oven for 25-30 minutes or until the pancetta is golden and the chicken cooked through.
