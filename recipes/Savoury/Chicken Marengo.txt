CHICKEN MARENGO

INGREDIENTS
- Oven 5/190°C/375°F

- 4 x skinless chicken breasts
- 3 or 4 slices bacon sliced
- 1/4lb (113g) mushrooms sliced
- 1 onion chopped
- 1 tablespoon flour seasoned with black pepper
- 1 tablespoon tomato puree
- mixed herbs
- 2 chicken oxos dissolved into ¾ pint hot water
- splash of sherry
- 1 tablespoon oil for frying chicken pieces

METHOD
1. Slice mushrooms and put in ovenproof dish
2. Coat chicken with flour and seal in pan for few minutes then put into dish with mushrooms
3. Fry onions and bacon in the same frying pan for few minutes and then add remainder of the flour to the frying pan with bacon and mushrooms, stir together and add tomato puree (mixture is quite thick at this stage)
4. Add hot chicken stock to the frying pan and add mixed herbs.   Bring to the boil.   Take off heat and add sherry.
5. Pour mixture over chicken and mushrooms, stir and then place into oven for approximately 11/2 hours.
6. Serve with rice or potatoes with vegetables
