Swiss Rosti Potato Cakes

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 4 spring onions, chopped
- 700g coarse grated, scrubbed raw potatoes
- 1tbsp plain flour
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped

METHOD
1. Add the spring onions to the potatoes. Mix in flour, eggs, chilli and seasoning.
2. Heat a griddle or non-stick frying pan and wipe with a little oil. Cook in batches: put spoonfuls of potato mixture on the griddle and fry until golden. Turn over and cook again - about 4 minutes each side.

NOTES
Not suitable for freezing
