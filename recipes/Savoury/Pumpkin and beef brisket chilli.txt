Slow-cooker pumpkin and beef brisket chilli

Serves: 8
Calories: 394 per serving

INGREDIENTS
- 20 minutes to prepare and 9 hrs to cook

- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 600g pumpkin, peeled, deseeded and cut into 2.5cm pieces
- 1kg beef brisket, string removed, patted dry with kitchen paper
- 2 onions, chopped
- 1 red pepper, roughly chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed​
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika​
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp fennel seeds, crushed
- 1 tbsp tomato purée​
- 75ml balsamic vinegar
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 beef stock pot, made up to 500ml
- 400g tin cannellini beans, drained​
- rice, to serve (optional)
- baked potato, to serve (optional)
- grated mature Cheddar, to serve (optional)
- 15g fresh coriander, leaves picked

- beef brisket (my joint was just under 1kg) salt and pepper garlic oil
- 4-6 carrots, chopped
- 3-4 potatoes, chopped
- 1-2 parsnips, chopped
- 650ml gf stock
- 4 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 3 tbsp gf soy sauce half tsp thyme
- 1 tsp garlic oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tbsp tomato puree
- 100g green beans

METHOD
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat and fry the pumpkin for 12-15 mins, tossing regularly, until browned. Tip into a bowl and set aside to cool; cover.
2. Season the beef brisket all over and place in the frying pan, fat-side down, for 12 mins, turning occasionally, until browned all over. Transfer to a plate and set aside.
3. Reduce the heat to medium, add the remaining oil and fry the onions and pepper for 5 mins, stirring regularly to scrape up any beef fat, until golden and caramelised. Add the garlic, spices and tomato purée and cook for 2 mins.
4. Pour in the vinegar and allow to mostly cook away for 1 min.
5. Tip into a 3.5ltr slow-cooker with the tinned tomatoes and stock. Add the brisket; cover and cook on low for 7 hrs.
6. Uncover the chilli and stir through the beans and pumpkin. Cover and cook for a further 1 hr 30 mins.
7. Shred the beef with 2 forks; season to taste. Serve with rice or baked potatoes and grated cheese, if you like, and scatter with the coriander to serve.
1) Season the beef with salt and pepper, then sear either in the slow cooker pan on the hob or a separate pan in a little garlic oil until sealed and coloured on all sides.
2) Place the carrots, potatoes and parsnips into the slow cooker, followed by the beef and all the other ingredients except the green beans.
3) Lid on and then cook on high for about 6 hours or low for closer to 8 hours.
4) 30 minutes before it finishes, add in the green beans to cook through.
5) To make the gravy bring all the juices to the boil and stir through cornflour mixed with a little water (1.5 tbsp cornflour mixed with 3 tbsp water), keep stirring and then simmer until it thickens to your liking facebook.com
