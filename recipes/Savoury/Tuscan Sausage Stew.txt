Tuscan Sausage Stew

Takes: 1hour   Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 1tbsp olive oil
- 6 sausages
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 large carrot, chopped
- 1 celery stick, chopped
- 1 medium red pepper, chopped
- 1 organic chicken stock cube
- 2 x 400g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 400g cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 1tbsp tomato puree
- 1 bay leaf
- 1tsp dried oregano
- 1tbsp pesto

METHOD
1. Pre-heat the oven to Gas 6, 200°C, fan 180°. Heat the oil in a casserole dish and brown the sausages for 5 minutes.
2. Add the onion, carrot, celery and pepper to soften. Add a little more oil if you need to and cook for 10 minutes.
3. Dissolve the stock cube in a mug (small!) of boiling water and add to the pot with the tinned tomatoes, cannellini beans, tomato puree, bay leaf and oregano. Bring to a simmer.
4. Cook in the oven with the lid on for 30-40 minutes. Stir in the pesto and serve immediately.
