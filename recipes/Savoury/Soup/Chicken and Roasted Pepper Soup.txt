Chicken and Roasted Pepper Soup

Serves: 6

INGREDIENTS
- 12 red peppers
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 10 chicken thighs, skin on
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1.5 litres / 2.5 pints warm chicken stock
- Handful of fresh basil leaves, roughly torn
- Handful of fresh mint leaves, roughly chopped
- 2 tsp fresh marjoram, chopped
- Duchese oil or good quality extra virgin olive oil

METHOD
1. Preheat the oven to 200ûC / 400ûF / Gas 6. Roast the peppers whole for 25 mins, then leave to cool. Scrape out the seeds and slice the peppers thinly.
2. Meanwhile, season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper and roast alongside the peppers for 20 mins, or until cooked. Cool briefly, then discard the skin and bones and shred the meat finely.
3. Heat the 2 tbsp oil in a large saucepan, add the onion and cook until golden. Add the strips of pepper and the garlic.
4. Add the stock to the pan, along with the chicken and herbs. Season to taste and simmer for 5 mins, then ladle into warmed soup bowls and serve with olive oil drizzled on top.
