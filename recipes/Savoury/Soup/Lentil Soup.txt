Lentil Soup

Serves: 3 adults

INGREDIENTS
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, peeled and crushed
- 2 medium carrots (approx 110g), cut into small chunks
- 100g butternut squash, peeled and diced
- 100g sweet potato, peeled and diced
- 200g celery, (2 stick), sliced
- 100g red lentils
- 750 ml vegetable stock
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 sprig of thyme
- half teaspoon salt and freshly ground black pepper

METHOD
1. Heat the vegetable oil in a fairly large pan, add the onion and garlic.
2. When soft, add the chopped vegetables and rinsed lentils. Pour over the vegetable stock and then cover and cook gently, stirring occasionally for 15 to 20 minutes or until the vegetables are soft. Scoop off any foam on te top
3. Add the stock, bay leaf, sprig of thyme and salt and pepper.
4. Simmer for 15 minutes.  Remove the bay leaf and thyme. Adjust the seasoning to taste.
