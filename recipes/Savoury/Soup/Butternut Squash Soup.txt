Butternut Squash Soup

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 1 butternut squash
- 2 large sweet potatoes
- 2 onions
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- 0.5 tsp mild chilli powder
- 900 ml chicken stock
- oil

METHOD
1. Fry onion until soft. Add garlic and spices and fry for a further 3 min. Add squash, sweet potato and stock and simmer for 20 - 30 mins until vegetables are cooked. Blend.
