Spiced Lentil, Chickpea and Chorizo Soup

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 100g Puy lentils
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 55g chorizo, chopped
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 3 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon
- 0.25 tsp crushed dried chilli
- 4 tomatoes, peeled, seeded and chopped
- 400g can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 1 tbsp tomato puree
- 1.2 l vegatable or chicken stock
- salt and ground black pepper
- juice of 0.5 lemon to taste

METHOD
1. Put the lentils in a large saucepan, pour in enough boiling water to cover them generously and simmer for about 20 minutes, until just tender. Drain well.
2. Heat the oil in the rinsed-out pan and gently fry the chorizo, onion and garlic or 4 minutes. Stir in the cumin, coriander, cinnamon and chilli, followed by the tomatoes, chickpeas, lentils, tomato puree and stock. Bring to the boil, reduce the heat, cover and simmer for 15 minutes.
3. Add salt and pepper and lemon juice to taste.

SOURCE
