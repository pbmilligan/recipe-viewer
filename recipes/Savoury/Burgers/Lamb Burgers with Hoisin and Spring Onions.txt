Lamb Burgers with Hoisin and Spring Onions

INGREDIENTS
- Makes 4 burgers

- 1lb minced lamb
- 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 4 spring onions, chopped
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh coriander
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh mint
- 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh ginger
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp mild chilli powder
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 1 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

METHOD
1. Place all the ingredients in a large bowl and, using your hands, mix until the ingredients are evenly incorporated. Divide the mixture into 4 burgers and grill for 5-7 minutes until cooked through and serve in a bun with salad, or as desired.

NOTES
I've changed the food names to British were appropriate (e.g. cilantro -> coriander). The original recipe had far more meat (1.5-1.75 lbw), which would have been too much for 4 burgers, so it's possible the seasonings should be toned down a bit. The original recipe suggested serving with rice.

SOURCE
Recipe of the Week: Burgers by Sally Sampson, p. 39
