Turkey, Camembert and Cranberry Burgers

INGREDIENTS
- Makes 4 burgers

- 1 lb minced turkey
- 4 oz Camembert
- Wild Cranberry chutney

METHOD
1. Divide the Camembert into four equal sizes and add to the burger maker when it is half-filled with the turkey mince, and then top up with the rest of the mince. Grill for 5-7 minutes until cooked through, top with the Cranberry chutney and serve in a bun with salad, or as desired.

NOTES
Original recipe proposed making the chutney yourself; we cheated and bought some in Tesco. Make sure that the Camembert is well contained in the turkey (if in doubt, use less cheese) otherwise it will all escape on to the grill pan.

SOURCE
Recipe of the Week: Burgers by Sally Sampson, p. 80
