Quarterpounder Beef Burgers

INGREDIENTS
- Makes 4 burgers

- 1 lb l/ 450g lean minced beef
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- salt and pepper

METHOD
1. Mix all the ingredients together in a bowl until thoroughly mixed. Divide the mixture into 4 burgers and grill for 5-7 minutes until cooked through and serve in a bun with salad, or as desired.
