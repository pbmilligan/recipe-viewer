Tartiflette (Potato Gratin)

Serves

INGREDIENTS
- 2 lb 12 oz / 1.25 kg medium Desiree potatoes, peeled and sliced 0.5 inch / 1 cm thick
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 12 slices streaky bacon, cut into chunks
- 2 large onions, peeled and finely slicked
- salt, pepper and fresh nutmeg
- 8 fl oz / 200 ml dry white wine
- 10.5 oz / 300g creme fraiche
- 1 large or 2 small Reblochon cheese

METHOD
1. Heat the oven to 200ûC / 400ûF / Gas 6. Simmer the potatoes in salted water until soft. Strain into a colander and leave to cool a little.
2. While potatoes are cooking, heat a large shallow pan with the vegetable oil and fry the bacon for 3 mins until the fat starts to run. Add the onion, cover and sweat for about 10 mins, stirring occasionally until soft. Turn up the heat and cook, stirring constantly for 5 mins or until golden, then drain on absorbent paper.
3. Cover the bottom of a gratin dish with half of the potatoes and season with salt, pepper and nutmeg. Spread the onion and bacon mixture over the top and follow with the second half of the potatoes. Pour in the white winaeand spread over the creme fraiche.
4. Slice the Reblochon in half widthways then slice each half through the centre. Place the four pieces of cheese, crust side up, over the top. Bake for 30 mins or until golden.

NOTES
Can substitute Camembert if can't find Reblochon.
