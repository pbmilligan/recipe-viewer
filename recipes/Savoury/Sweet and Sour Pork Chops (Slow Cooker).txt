Sweet and Sour Pork Chops (slow cooker)

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 1tbsp plain flour
- Salt and pepper
- 4 lean pork chops, trimmed of excess fat
- 2tbsp oil
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 90ml vegetable stock
- 60ml soy sauce
- 4tbsp tomato puree
- 4tbsp soft brown sugar
- 60ml dry sherry
- Plain rice, to serve

METHOD
1. 1 Season the flour with salt and pepper, then dust the chops with the flour, shaking off any excess. 2 Heat the oil in a frying pan and brown the chops quickly on all sides. Transfer to the slow cooker. 3 Add the onions to the pan and cook for about five minutes, stirring occasionally, until beginning to turn golden brown. Stir in the remaining ingredients and bring just to the boil. Pour over the chops in the slow cooker. 4 Cover and cook on LOW for 5-8 hours.

NOTES
Doubled up the quantities.

SOURCE
New Recipes For Your Slo-Cooker by Annette Yates, p88
