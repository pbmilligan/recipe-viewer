Easy Bolognaise Sauce

INGREDIENTS
- Makes 15 portions

- 5tbsp olive oil
- 4 onions, finely chopped
- 4 carrots, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2kg minced beef (or 1kg minced beef and 1kg minced veal)
- 500ml red wine
- 4x400g tins chopped tomatoes
- 500ml beef stock
- 2tbsp granulated sugar
- 200g tomato puree
- 1tsp dried oregano
- 8 sprigs thyme
- 1tsp ground nutmeg

METHOD
1. Heat the olive oil in a large saucepan and gently saute the onion and carrot for 8-10 minutes, or until tender and slightly coloured.
2. Add the garlic and the minced meat and cook, stirring, until the meat starts to brown. Break up any lumps of meat with a fork.
3. Add the red wine and cook, stirring frequently, for about 5 minutes until half of the wine has evaporated. Add the remaining ingredients. Simmer (uncovered), stirring occasionally, over low heat for 1.5 hours, or until reduced and slightly thickened. Season to taste.
4. When the bolognaise has cooled, freeze into single or double-sized portions and keep for up to 3 months.

NOTES
Halved the quantities - produced six generous portions
