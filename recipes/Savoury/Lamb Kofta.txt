Lamb Kofta

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 500g lamb mince
- 1 finely grated onion
- handful chopped parsley
- zest of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp Ground Cumin
- 1 tsp Spanish Paprika
- 1 tsp Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- 2/3 tsp Ground Coriander
- 2/3 tsp Freshly Grated Nutmeg
- 2/3 tsp Ground Cinnamon

- Pound mince with fist to break it up.
- squeeze juice out of onion and discard.

METHOD
1. Mix everything together. Shape into egg shapes, thread 2 skewers through and shape around them. Grill.
