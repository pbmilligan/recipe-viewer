Chilli Con Carne

Serves: 8

INGREDIENTS
- 750g Minced Beef
- 2-3 Onions, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 x 400g Tinned Tomatoes
- Tomato puree 2-3 tbsp
- worcester sauce  1-2 tsp

METHOD
Chilli powder 3 tsp/1 tbsp (originally 1.5 tsp) (correct amount for everyone with Extra Hot Chilli powder) 2 x 400g tin Red kidney beans try with paprika, carrots, celery, peppers too
1. Fry onions, garlic and mince until the beef is browned and the onions are soft.
2. Add the chilli powder and fry for a further 2 minutes
3. Add the beans and tomatoes, cover and simmer for at least 30 minutes.
