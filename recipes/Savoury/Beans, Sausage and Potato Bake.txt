Beans, Sausage and Potato Bake

Serves: 2

INGREDIENTS
- 415g Baked Beans with Sausages
- 2 large potatoes, cooked
- 25 g butter
- 50 ml milk
- 25 g Cheddar cheese, grated
- Black pepper

METHOD
1. Pre-heat the oven to 180 degC.
2. Mash potato with butter, milk, cheese and pepper.
3. Put beans and sausages into oven proof dish and put mash over top.
4. Bake for 20 mins until golden brown.

SOURCE
Heinz Beans tin
