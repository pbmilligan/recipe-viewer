Cheeky Chicken and Potato One Pot

Serves: 4-6

INGREDIENTS
- 500g chicken thighs, cut into large chunks
- 600g pumpkin or squash, peeled, deseeded and cut into 4cm chunks
- 600g Desiree potatoes, peeled and cut into 4cm chunks
- 150g green beans, trimmed and halved
- Small handful of basil leaves, roughly torn
- 2tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 large onion, roughly chopped
- 2-3tbsp red curry paste
- 400g reduced-fat tinned coconut milk
- 350ml hot chicken stock

METHOD
1. Heat oil in a very large pan or flameproof casserole dish. Add the chicken and onion, and cook for 10 minutes, stirring until the chicken is golden. Stir in the curry paste and cook for a further minute before adding the pumpkin and potatoes.
2. Add the coconut milk and stock, bring to the boil, cover loosely and simmer for 5 minutes; uncover and cook for a further 10 minutes. Stir in the beans and cook for 4 minutes until they are almost tender. Stir through the basil, remove from the heat and leave to infuse for a few minutes before serving.

NOTES

Used butternut squash and full-fat coconut milk

SOURCE
Essentials magazine
