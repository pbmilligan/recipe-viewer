Chili Chocolate Bread

Prep Time: 20 minutes minutes
Cook Time: 30 minutes minutes
Total time: 1 hour hour 50 minutes minutes
Serves: 8 servings
Calories: 232kcal

INGREDIENTS
- Proofing 1 hour hour

- 3 cups bread flour
- 2 tablespoons white sugar or honey
- 2 teaspoons fast-action yeast
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1 cup cool water
- 1 teaspoon red chili flakes
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup cocoa powder

METHOD
1. In a mixer bowl add the warm water, honey (or sugar), salt, yeast, and a few tablespoons of flour.
2. Stir the mixture and set aside for 15 minutes to activate the yeast.
3. Add the remaining flour, sugar, cocoa, and chili flakes then place on the mixer with a dough hook and mix for 10 minutes.
4. Remove and shape the dough and place into a loaf tin and leave in a warm place for 1 hour to rise to double in size.
5. Pre-heat oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit and bake for 30-35 minutes until turning dark on top.
6. Allow to cool for 20 minutes before slicing and serving with some butter.
