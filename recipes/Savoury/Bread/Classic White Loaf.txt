ALLINSON CLASSIC WHITE BREAD

INGREDIENTS
- extra large
- 1.5 cups (345 ml) Warm Water
- 3 tbsp (35g) Soft Butter
- 2 tbsp Granulated Sugar
- 2 tsp Salt
- 4 cups (630g) Allinson Strong White Bread Flour
- 2 tsp* Allinson Easy Bake Yeast

- large
- 1 cup (225 ml) Warm Water
- 2 tbsp (25g) Soft Butter
- 1.5 tbsp Granulated Sugar
- 1.5 tsp Salt
- 3 cups (450g) Allinson Strong White Bread Flour
- 1.25 tsp* Allinson Easy Bake Yeast

BREAD MAKER METHOD:
- Put the ingredients into the bread maker baking pan in the order specified for your particular model (see

METHOD
1. manufacturer's handbook). Set to the Normal/Basic White Program for the appropriate size loaf.
2. TRADITIONAL METHOD: * If you want your loaf to prove faster, use 2.5 tsp (1 sachet) of Allinson Easy Bake Yeast (see page 32). Put the flour, salt, sugar, butter and yeast into a bowl. Mix to a soft dough with the water. Turn onto a floured surface and knead for 10 minutes. Shape as desired and put into an appropriate greased tin. Leave to prove slowly until double in size. Add your chosen topping/glaze if using (see page 16) and place in an oven preheated to 220°C/425°F/Gas Mark 7 and immediately turn the heat down to 200°C/400°F/Gas Mark 6. Bake for 25-30 minutes. When cooked the loaf should sound hollow when tapped on the bottom.
