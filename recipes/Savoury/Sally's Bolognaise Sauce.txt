Sally’s Bolognaise Sauce

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- Vegetable oil
- 1 onion
- 1 large carrot
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 450g minced beef
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- Splash Worcestershire sauce

METHOD
1. Heat oil and fry onions and carrots for 5-10 minutes, until softened.
2. Tip onto a plate and then fry the mince until browned; add the Worcestershire sauce and garlic for the last couple of minutes.
3. Re-add the onions and carrots and add the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée and oregano.
4. Bring to the boil and then leave to simmer for at least 30 minutes. Use a lid as appropriate.
5. Serve with pasta.

SOURCE
Sally (Paul added the carrots and Worcestershire sauce)
