Preparation 25 minutes, Cooking 1.5 hours

INGREDIENTS
- Makes 8-10 portions

Prepare:
- scrub potato, carrot

METHOD
chop leek, celery, peel garlic 4tbsp olive oil 4 leeks, cut into 1cm thick slices 6 rashers streaky bacon, chopped 3 stalks celery, sliced 500g baby or Chantenay carrots, scrubbed 8 large chicken thighs 4 chicken breasts, halved 3 fresh bay leaves 4 cloves garlic, peeled and left whole 1l chicken stock Fresh thyme (optional)
1. Heat the oven to 180degC. Heat the olive oil over a medium heat in a large frying pan or a large flameproof casserole dish or saucepan. Sauté the leeks, bacon, and celery for 5-6 minutes, or until they have softened (without browning too much). Add the carrots, chicken pieces, bay leaves, garlic cloves, and stock. If you do have some fresh thyme, add a few sprigs of it to the mixture.
2. Bring to the boil, cover with a tight-fitting lid and transfer to the oven. Cook for 1 hour. Remove the lid and cook for a further 30 minutes, or until the chicken is nicely coloured, then serve.

NOTES
Spoon picture. Can make half quantity.
Halved quantities. Served with new potatoes.

2tbsp olive oil
2 leeks, cut into 1cm thick slices
3 rashers streaky bacon, chopped
2 stalks celery, sliced
250g baby or Chantenay carrots, scrubbed
1kg skin on chicken thighs
2 fresh bay leaves
2 cloves garlic, peeled and left whole
500ml chicken stock
Fresh thyme (optional)
