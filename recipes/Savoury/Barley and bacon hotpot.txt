Barley and bacon hotpot

Serves: 4
Takes: 1.5 hours

INGREDIENTS
- 25g butter
- 250g streaky bacon, derinded and finely chopped
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 250g carrots, thinly sliced
- 175g celery, chopped
- 100g mushrooms, thinly sliced (or chopped tiny so Sam doesn't notice)
- 250g pearl barley
- 600ml chicken stock
- 4tbsp chopped parsley
- salt and pepper

METHOD
1. Heat the oven to 180 degC
2. Melt the butter in a flameproof casserole. Add the bacon and onions and fry gently for 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the vegetables and pearl barley, then the stock. Bring to the boil. Add the parsley and season.
4. Cover and bake for 1 hour, or until the barley is soft and nearly all the liquid has been absorbed. Serve hot.

SOURCE
One-pot Cooking, p. 42.
