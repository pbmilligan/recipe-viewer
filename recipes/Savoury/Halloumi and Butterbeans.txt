Halloumi and Butterbeans

INGREDIENTS
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 red onion, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 jar (400g) roasted red peppers, sliced
- 2 tins (800g) chopped tomatoes
- 1 jar (600g) butter beans, drained
- 1 packet (225g) halloumi, sliced
- 2 large handfuls fresh spinach

METHOD
1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe pan over medium heat. Sauté onion for 8 minutes.
3. Add garlic, paprika, chili flakes, and oregano; cook briefly.
4. Stir in peppers and tomatoes; simmer for a few minutes.
5. Mix in butter beans and top with halloumi slices.
6. Bake for 20 minutes.
7. Stir in spinach and let it wilt before serving.
