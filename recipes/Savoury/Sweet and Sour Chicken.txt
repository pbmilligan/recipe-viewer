Sweet and Sour Chicken

Serves: 4
Serve: d with rice. We also added 2 carrots at the same time as the pineapple chunks.

INGREDIENTS
- 25g/1oz margarine or butter
- 1 red pepper, seeded and cut into chunks
- 1 bunch spring onions, chopped
- 4 tbsp soft dark brown sugar
- 2 tsp cornflour
- 1 chicken stock cube
- 350g/12oz can pineapple chunks, drained and syrup reserved
- 2 tbsp wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- salt (optional)
- 225-350g/8-12oz cooked chicken, cut into chunks

METHOD
1. Heat the margarine or butter in a saucepan, add the red pepper and chopped spring onions and fry gently for 3-4 minutes until the vegetables are soft but not browned. Put the soft dark brown sugar, cornflour and chicken stock cube into a measuring jug and slowly stir in boiling water up to the 300ml/0.5pt mark. Continue stirring until the ingredients have dissolved. Add the reserved pineapple syrup, vinegar and soy sauce. Season to taste with salt. Pour this liquid into the pan with the red pepper and spring onions and bring to the boil. Lower the heat and simmer the mixture for about 2 minutes, stirring. Add the chicken and pineapple chunks and continue to simmer the curry over gentle heat for about 10 minutes. Serve hot.
