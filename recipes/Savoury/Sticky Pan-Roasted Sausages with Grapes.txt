Roasted Sausages with Grapes

Serves: 4

INGREDIENTS
- 1tbsp Olive Oil
- 450g Pork Sausages
- 2 onions, sliced
- 1 garlic clove, sliced
- 1tsp Fennel seeds
- 100g South African grapes
- 2tbsp red wine vinegar

METHOD
1. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over a medium heat, add the sausages and cook for 10 minutes, turning every so often
2. Stir in the onions and leave to cook for 5 more minutes until the sausages are browned and the onions are softened
3. Add the garlic, fennel seeds and grapes and cook for 5 more minutes, stirring regularly, until the grapes are starting to soften.
4. Pour over the vinegar and swirl around the pan
5. Cook for a few more minutes until the onions are sticky and the sausages cooked throughout
6. Ideal served with mashed potatoes

NOTES

We did serve it with mash, but we used white wine vinegar and non-South African grapes (gasp!)
