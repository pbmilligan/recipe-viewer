Chicken and Blue Chesse Pasta

Serves: 1

INGREDIENTS
- 1 clove garlic, chopped
- ½ courgette, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- ½ tomato, thinly sliced
- ½ glass white wine
- ½ chicken stock cube
- 55g/2oz chicken, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp mustard
- 55g/2oz blue cheese
- 85ml/3fl oz double cream
- 140g/5oz pasta, cooked

- Sauté the garlic and courgette in oil for two to three minutes.

METHOD
1. Add the tomato, wine, chicken stock cube, chicken trimmings and mustard. Simmer for four to five minutes. Roughly blend the mixture but leave it a bit chunky. Crumble in the cheese and add the cream. Simmer for one minute. Stir in the pasta and serve.
