Shepherd's Pie

Serves: 3-4

INGREDIENTS
- 25g butter
- 1 large onion (120g), peeled and chopped
- 2 carrots, peeled and chopped
- 1tbsp flour
- 300ml Beef Stock
- 1tbsp tomato puree
- Worcester sauce
- 0.5tsp mixed dry herbs
- Salt and pepper
- 450g minced lamb
- 600g potatoes, cut into 1cm slices
- Butter and milk

METHOD
add 120g red or brown lentils, cooked according to the packet
1. Peel and boil the potatoes for 20 minutes until they soften.
2. Meanwhile, fry the minced lamb and put aside.
3. Meanwhile, cook the lentils. To cook red lentils on the hob, use 300ml water for 120g of dry lentils, bring the pot to a boil, and then simmer gently for 10 to 15 minutes until tender.
4. Melt the butter and fry the onion and carrots until brown. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually stir in the stock and bring to the boil, stirring constantly.
5. Preheat the oven to 180degC.
6. Add the lentils, tomato puree, Worcester sauce, herbs and seasoning. Cover the pan, reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes. Mash the potatoes with the butter and milk.
7. Remove from the heat and add the minced meat, mix well and turn into an ovenproof dish. Allow to cool a little before covering with the mashed potato.
8. Transfer the dish to the oven and heat for 10-20 minutes, until the potatoes is starting to crisp.

NOTES
Add 120g lentils to make it serve 4
The pan is hot when you remove it from the oven!!!
