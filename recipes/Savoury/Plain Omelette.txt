Plain Omelette

Serves: 1

INGREDIENTS
- 3 Eggs
- Salt
- Pepper
- 20g Butter

METHOD
1. Break the eggs into a small bowl, add a teaspoon of cold water, salt and pepper and half of the butter. Beat them a little with a fork. Melt the rest of the butter in a small frying pan, or omelette pan if you have one. It will start to sizzle and then to foam; just as the butter starts to smell nutty, swoosh in the egg. Quickly stir the egg three or four times with the fork, it will start to set on the bottom of the pan. Lift the edge of the omelette nearest the handle with the fork, tilting the pan as you do, and gently flip one half of the omelette over the other. Slide out the finished omelette on to a warm plate.

NOTES

Add grated cheese, ham and chopped parsley

SOURCE
"Real Fast Food", Nigel Slater (p.52)
