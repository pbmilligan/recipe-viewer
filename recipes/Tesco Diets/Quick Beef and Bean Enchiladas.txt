Quick beef & bean enchiladas

Serve: sweetcorn, lettuce, tomato, cheese, jalapeños

INGREDIENTS
- 75 g. of lean minced beef
- 1 227g tin of chopped tomatoes
- 1 tbs of tomato-based salsa
- 1 pinch of chilli powder
- 1 flour tortilla
- 50 g. of re-fried beans

METHOD
1. Heat a non-stick frying pan and cook the beef over a medium heat until browned.
2. Drain off excess fat and stir in the tomatoes, salsa and chilli, bring to a boil and simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Spread the tortilla(s) with the re-fried beans, then spoon the beef down the centre of the tortilla.
4. Roll up and cook for 3-4 minutes on medium heat in the microwave. Alternatively, heat through at 180 degrees for 15 minutes.
