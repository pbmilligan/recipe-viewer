Italian sausage in red wine and cannellini beans

METHOD
1. Fry 8 good quality Italian sausages until nicely browned, set aside.
2. Fried 2 chopped onions and 3 cloves of crushed garlic in 3 tablespoons of olive oil for five minutes.
3. Add 2×400 g tins of drained cannellini beans, a glass of red wine and 2×400 g of chopped tomatoes, bring to a simmer.
4. Slice each of the sausages in half and add to the pan with 5 chopped fresh sage leaves and continue to simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Finally season with sea salt and milled pepper, and finish with chopped parsley.

SOURCE
tin of Cannellini beans (Salsiccia al vino rosso e cannellini)
