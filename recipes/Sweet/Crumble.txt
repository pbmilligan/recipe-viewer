Crumble

Serves: ??

INGREDIENTS
For the crumble topping:
- 150g plain flour
- 75g caster sugar
- 75g unsalted butter at room temperature, cut into pieces
- scatter with sugar on top once in dish

For apple crumble filling:
- 450g/1lb apples, peeled, cored and cut into 1cm/½in pieces
- 50g/2oz unrefined brown sugar
- 1 tbsp plain flour
- 1 pinch of ground cinnamon

For rhubarb crumble filling:

METHOD
1. roast the rhubarb: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/roasted-rhubarb 550g rhubarb 80g caster sugar Roast 180C 15-20 min covered in a single layer and covered in foil
2. Nice with blueberries added after roasting but before baking crumble Bake crumble 20 min
