Ice Cream - Sweet Cream Base 1

INGREDIENTS
- Makes 1 quart

- 2 large eggs
- 0.75 cups sugar
- 2 cups heavy or whipping cream
- 1 cup milk

METHOD
1. Whisk the eggs in a mixing bowl until light and fluffy, 1 to 2 minutes. Whisk in the sugar, a little at a time, then continue whisking until completely blended, about 1 minute more. Pour in the cream and milk and whisk to blend.

NOTES

Especially good as a background for fruit, cookies and candy.
Usually halve the quantities.

SOURCE
Ben & Jerry's Homemade Ice Cream & Dessert Book, Ben Cohen & Jerry Greenfield with Nancy J. Stevens (p.28)
