Banana Peanut Butter Ice Cream

INGREDIENTS
- Makes generous 1 quart

- Sweet Cream Base 1
- 0.5 cup smooth peanut butter
- 2 overripe bananas
- Juice of 1 lemon

METHOD
1. Prepare the Sweet Cream Base. Pour about 1 cup of the cream base into a separate bowl and stir in the peanut butter, a little at a time. Return to the remaining cream base and stir until completely blended. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's instructions. Mash the bananas in a bowl, add the lemon juice, and whisk until smooth. After the ice cream stiffens (about 2 minutes before it is done), add the banana mixture, then continue freezing until the ice cream is ready.

NOTES

Halved quantities

SOURCE
Ben & Jerry's Homemade Ice Cream & Dessert Book, Ben Cohen & Jerry Greenfield with Nancy J. Stevens (p.63)
