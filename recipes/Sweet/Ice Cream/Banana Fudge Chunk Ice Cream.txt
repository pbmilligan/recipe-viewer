Banana Fudge Chunk Ice Cream

INGREDIENTS
- Makes generous 1 quart

- Sweet Cream Base 3 (2 cups whipping cream; 1 cup condensed milk)
- 2 overripe bananas
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 cup cold coarsely chopped chocolate fudge

METHOD
1. Prepare the Sweet Cream Base. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's instructions.
2. Mash the bananas and lemon juice together in a bowl, then whisk until smooth.
3. Stir fudge into the banana mixture.
3. After the ice cream stiffens (about 2 minutes before it is done), add the banana mixture, then continue freezing until the ice cream is ready.

NOTES

Used six fingers of Fudge

SOURCE
Ben & Jerry's Homemade Ice Cream & Dessert Book, Ben Cohen & Jerry Greenfield with Nancy J. Stevens (p.52)
