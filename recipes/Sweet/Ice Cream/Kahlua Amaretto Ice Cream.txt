Kahlua Amaretto Ice Cream

INGREDIENTS
- Makes generous 1 quart

- Sweet Cream Base 1
- 0.25 cup Kahlua
- 0.25 cup Amaretto liqueur

- Prepare the Sweet Cream Base. Add the liqueurs and blend.

METHOD
1. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's instructions.

NOTES

Halved quantities

SOURCE
Ben & Jerry's Homemade Ice Cream & Dessert Book, Ben Cohen & Jerry Greenfield with Nancy J. Stevens (p.68)
