Coconut Rum Ice Cream

INGREDIENTS
- Makes generous 1 quart

- Sweet Cream Base 1
- 1 can (15 ounces) coconut cream
- 2 tbsp dark rum

METHOD
1. Prepare the Sweet Cream Base. Add the coconut cream and the rum and blend.
2. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's instructions.

NOTES

Used a tin of coconut cream from Tesco.

SOURCE
Ben & Jerry's Homemade Ice Cream & Dessert Book, Ben Cohen & Jerry Greenfield with Nancy J. Stevens (p.67)
