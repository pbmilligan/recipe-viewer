Liquorice Ice Cream

Serves: 6

INGREDIENTS
- 150g liquorice
- 1 cup milk
- 300ml whipping cream
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 4 egg yolks

METHOD
1. Combine liquorice, milk and cream in medium saucepan. Stir over medium heat without boiling, until liquorice has dissolved. Bring just to the boil and remove from the heat. Beat sugar and egg yolks in a small bowl with electric mixer until thick and creamy. Beat hot milk mixture gradually into egg yolk mixture. Cool. Freeze in ice cream maker according to manufacturers instructions.

NOTES

It's a pain to get the liquorice to dissolve in the hot milk
