Lemon Curd Ice Cream

Serves: 6

INGREDIENTS
- 4 egg yolks
- 180g caster sugar
- 40g butter, cut into bits
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 250ml milk
- 375ml single cream

METHOD
1. Bring a pan half-full of water to the boil and then lower the heat to the absolute minimum.
2. Put the egg yolks and sugar in a wide glass or stainless steel bowl and whisk until they are thick and creamy.
3. Sit the bowl on top of the pan of water, add the butter and let it melt. Whisk until it starts to get thicker and creamier.
4. Add the lemon zest and juice and carry on whisking until it thickens.
5. Meanwhile, warm up the milk a little. Whisk it into the lemony eggs and then remove the bowl from the heat.
6. Whisk from time to time until it's cool before you whisk in the cream.
7. Put the bowl in the fridge until it's cool and then transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's instructions.

NOTES
25 minutes in ice cream maker.

SOURCE
Daily Mail (Weekend magazine), May or June 2006
