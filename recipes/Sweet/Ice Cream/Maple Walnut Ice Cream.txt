Maple Walnut Ice Cream

INGREDIENTS
- Makes generous 1 quart

- Sweet Cream Base 1
- 0.25 cup maple syrup
- 0.5 cup coarsely chopped walnuts

- Prepare the Sweet Cream Base. Add the maple syrup and stir until blended.

METHOD
1. Transfer the mixture to an ice cream maker and freeze following the manufacturer's instructions. After the ice cream stiffens (about 2 minutes before it is done), add the walnuts, then continue freezing until the ice cream is ready.

NOTES

Halved quantities

SOURCE
Ben & Jerry's Homemade Ice Cream & Dessert Book, Ben Cohen & Jerry Greenfield with Nancy J. Stevens (p.70)
