Cupcakes

INGREDIENTS
Cupcakes:

- 100g/4 oz softened unsalted butter
- 100g/4 oz self raising flour
- 100g/4 oz caster sugar
- 2 eggs

Cream cheese frosting:

- 300g icing sugar
- 50g softened unsalted butter
- 125g cream cheese

- Makes 12 cupcakes

- Turn on the oven to Gas 4/180 C/170C fan.

METHOD
1. In a large bowl, whisk all the ingredients until creamy. Spoon into individual cupcake cases and cook for about 10 - 15 minutes until cooked.
2. To check they are cooked just press gently and they should spring back.
3. Once cooled, the cakes can be decorated with cream cheese frosting (or icing) and then decorated.
4. To make the frosting, put the icing sugar and butter in a bowl. Using an electric whisk, beat together until well mixed.
5. Add all the cream cheese to the mixture and using the whisk mix all together until the frosting is light & fluffy. Be careful not to overmix or it can quickly become runny.
6. Spread onto the cupcakes with a flat knife or pipe on using a piping bag. To change the colour of the frosting, simple add a drop or two of food colouring.
7. Chocolate cupcakes: To make chocolate cupcakes, replace 25g of flour with cocoa powder.
