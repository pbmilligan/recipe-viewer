Florentines

INGREDIENTS
- 2oz butter
- 2oz golden syrup
- 2oz Demerara sugar
- 20z chopped nuts
- 2oz chopped glace cherries
- 2oz candied peel
- 6oz dark chocolate

METHOD
1. In a saucepan gently melt the butter, demerara sugar and golden syrup. Remove from the heat.
2. Next stir in the chopped nuts, chopped glacé cherries and candied peel. Add 2 oz plain flour to the mixture and stir well. Spoon teaspoonfuls of the mixture onto lined baking sheets.
3. Space them well apart as they will spread a lot.
4. Cook for 8 – 10 minutes at 180
5. Remove from oven and leave to harden.
6. Melt the chocolate over a pan of hot water and spread on reverse of Florentines.
7. Leave to cool and for the chocolate to harden.
8. Eat!
9. (Best stored in fridge if they last that long!)
10. (It was suggested that chopped preserved ginger could be used instead of or in addition to the glace cherries or nuts but we haven’t tried that – yet!)
