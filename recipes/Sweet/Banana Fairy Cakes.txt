INGREDIENTS
- 100g/4oz self raising flour
- 100g/4oz butter/margarine
- 100g/4oz soft brown sugar
- 1 old banana that has been left in the bowl too long

METHOD
1. Put oven on to 200 C/Gas 6.
2. Line a bun tin with cup-cake-cases.
3. Put all the ingredients into the food processor or hand whisk (best to start with butter & sugar first, then add the rest gradually) until all ingredients are in and smooth and feel light & airy.
4. Put a generous portion of mixture into each cup-cake-cases.
5. Pop into oven for between 12-20 mins depending on your oven!

NOTES
(from Clare) I usually add a few chocolate drops to each cup-cake before putting in the oven, but you can leave plain, and sprinkle with icing sugar when they come out or just have them straight!
Delicious with ice-cream too.
