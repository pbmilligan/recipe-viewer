Highland Flummery

Serves: ?

INGREDIENTS
- 3 tablespoons of whisky
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons of clear honey
- Half a pint (300 ml) double cream
- Medium oatmeal or digestive biscuits

METHOD
1. Blend the whisky, lemon juice and honey together to thin the honey. Add the double cream and whisk until thick and light. Spoon into individual serving glasses. If you have whisked it really well the mixture should mound softly in each glass. Chill for several hours. Sprinkle with oatmeal or crushed digestives.
