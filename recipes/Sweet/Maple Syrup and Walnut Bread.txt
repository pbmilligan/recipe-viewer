Maple Syrup and Walnut Bread

INGREDIENTS
- 1 cup water, tepid
- 1 tbsp dried skimmed milk
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 tbsp maple syrup at room temperature
- 2 cups wholewheat bread flour
- 1 cup white bread flour
- 1 tsp yeast
- 3 ounces walnuts, roughly chopped

METHOD
1. Place the ingredients in the order given in your bread machine's instructions.
2. Add the walnuts when your machine beeps which is usually after the first five minutes when you add items like raisins.
