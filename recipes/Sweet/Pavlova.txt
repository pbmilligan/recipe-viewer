Pavlova

Serves: 6

INGREDIENTS
- 4 egg whites
- 8oz caster sugar (227g)
- 0.5 tsp vanilla essence
- 1 tsp cornflour
- 1 tsp vinegar

METHOD
1. Beat egg whites until stiff, add sugar and cornflour and beat again.
2. Add vinegar and vanilla essence and beat.
3. Turn into a 10" oiled tin.
4. Bake on shelf 2 at 140degC for 1 hour.
