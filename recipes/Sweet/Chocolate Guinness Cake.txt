Chocolate Guinness Cake

Serves: ?

INGREDIENTS
Cake:
- 4 oz margarine
- 10 oz dark soft brown sugar
- 2 medium eggs, beater
- 6 oz plain flour
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- a level tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 7 fl oz Guinness
- 2 oz cocoa
Icing:
- 4 oz plain chocolate
- 2 tbsp Guinness
- 2 oz butter
- 4 oz icing sugar, sifted
- 1 oz walnuts, finely chopped

Cake:

METHOD
1. Grease and line two 8 inch sandwich tins, and preheat the oven to gas 4 / 180û C.
2. Cream butter and sugar together. Gradually beat in the eggs.
3. Sift the flour, baking powder and bicarbonate of soda into a separate bowl.
4. Stir Guinness into cocoa. Carefully fold in alternate quantities of flour and coca to butter/sugar mixture.
5. Spread mixture into tins and bake for 30-35 minutes. Leave to cool in tins for 5 mins before turning out. Icing:
1. Melt chocolate with Guinness, then beat in butter and leave to cool a little before blending in icing sugar.
2. Remove 1/4 of icing and stir in chopped walnuts. When icing has cooled to spreading consistency, sandwich cake with walnut icing and use rest for top and sides.
3. Decorate with walnut halves and grated chocolate.
