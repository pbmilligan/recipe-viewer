Grandma’s Raspberry Jam

Take: jam jar?

INGREDIENTS
- Or for recipe with jam sugar see https://www.bakingmad.com/recipes/raspberry-jam

Makes 10lb of jam (10 x 454g jars):
- 4lb /1.8kg raspberries
- 5 1/2lb / 2.5kg granulated sugar
- 1 bottle Certo (if making smaller quantity, a bottle is use within 14 days)

METHOD
1. Put the berries in  a pan with the sugar and crush (I use my potato masher).  I use homegrown raspberries which are probably riper and juicier than supermarket ones and crush easily.
2. Heat gently, stirring occasionally until the sugar has dissolved
3. Bring quickly to a full rolling boil and boil rapidly for 2 minutes stirring occasionally to stop it burning on the bottom of the pan
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the Certo
5. This recipe is for raspberry jam complete with pips so I sieve it by putting a metal sieve over a bowl and pouring the jam through using a spoon to push through the flesh Then pour into sterilized jam jars using a jam funnel
6. To sterilise jars wash and put in oven on 120 degrees for about 20 minutes (do this while making the jam)
