Ginger biscuits

INGREDIENTS
- makes about 12 biscuits

- 4oz plain flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 2oz granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon bicarb of soda
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 2oz Marge or butter
- 2oz golden syrup

METHOD
1. With exception of golden syrup, mix all above ingredients into breadcrumbs.
2. Add the 2oz golden syrup to mixture and mix using a spoon, mix into a dough.
3. Take generous teaspoons of the mixture and hand roll into balls.
4. Place on baking tray lined with grease proof paper.
5. Bake for 11 mins in preheated oven, 180°C in fan or gas oven or 200°C in a conventional oven.
6. Once cooked leave to stand on tray for 5 mins where they will harden.
