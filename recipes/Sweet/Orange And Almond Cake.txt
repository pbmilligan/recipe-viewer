Orange And Almond Cake

Serves: 6

INGREDIENTS
- 2 large oranges
- 6 eggs
- 250g ground almonds
- 250g golden caster sugar
- 1 level tsp baking powder

METHOD
1. Wash and boil the whole, unpeeled oranges in a moderate amount of water for two hours, or until they are very tender. Drain and cool them. Then remove the pips and blitz the fruit to a pulp. If you do not have a food processor, you could push the oranges through a sieve.
2. Set the oven to 190degC/375degF/gas mark 5. Grease and line an 8.5in/20cm springform cake tin.
3. Beat the eggs in a large mixing bowl and fold in all of the other ingredients, including the oranges.
4. Pour the mixture into the cake tin and bake for an hour. In modern, fan-assisted, ovens this might be plenty of time. But if the cake still feels very wobbly give it longer, checking it at 10-minute intervals.
5. Allow the cake to cool thoroughly before turning it out to serve.

NOTES
Cooked for an hour - slightly overdone on the outside, but very nice in the middle
