Raspberry and Vanilla Lollies

Serves: 4
Prep time: 10mins + 4-5hrs freezing time

INGREDIENTS
- 200 g (7oz) fresh raspberries
- 2 tbsp caster sugar
- 2 tbsp orange juice
- 150 ml (5fl oz) smooth vanilla yogurt

METHOD
1. Mash the raspberries with a fork until smooth, then add the sugar and orange juice and stir.
2. Divide half the mixture between 4 ice lolly moulds. Wipe the sides of the moulds clean then add a generous tablespoon of yogurt on top of each layer of raspberries. Top with a further layer of raspberries.
3. Add lolly sticks and freeze for 4-5 hours or overnight until firm.
4. Run lolly moulds under a hot tap and the lollies will slip out easily.
